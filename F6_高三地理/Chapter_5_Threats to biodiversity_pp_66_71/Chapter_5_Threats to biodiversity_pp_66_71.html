2 • Nutrient Cycling & Gersmehl Diagrams (養分循環與三大氣候區 Gersmehl 因果機制 pp.67–68)

Figure 5.2 : How Original Climate & Biome-Specific Human Disturbances Shape B, L & S

The cells of all organisms are made up of hydrogen (H), oxygen (O), carbon (C), nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and sulphur (S)—with the first four making up ~99% of almost all cells (plus calcium and ~24 elements in total). In Phil Gersmehl’s (1976) model (Figure 5.2), the size of each nutrient store circle (, , Soil S) is controlled by the relative speed of the inflow and outflow arrows—which in turn are governed by temperature (T) and precipitation (P) (學生理解關鍵：為什麼不同氣候區的 B、L、S 圓圈大小完全不同？因為「原始氣候的氣溫與降水」決定了分解者活性、植物根系吸收與雨水淋溶的「箭頭粗細速率」，進而決定養分會堆積在哪一個儲存庫中！).

💧 2. Water Footprint & River Diversion (水足跡、蒸發流失與印染污染 pp.70–71)

Making a single cotton T-shirt and pair of jeans consumes 20,000 litres (20 m³) of freshwater, while global fabric dyeing uses 5 trillion litres of water each year. Surface and groundwaters are diverted to irrigate cotton in dry picking climates—causing severe freshwater loss via evaporation, inefficient management, and toxic fertiliser/pesticide runoff (1套T恤牛仔褲耗水2萬公升，全球印染年耗水5兆公升；引水灌溉造成嚴重蒸發流失與農藥徑流污染).

🏭 3. Carbon Emissions & Habitat / Soil Degradation (碳排放與棲地土壤退化 pp.70–71)

Producing and transporting one cotton shirt releases 2.1 kg of CO₂ (with the average person buying 60 garments/yr, and affluent consumers buying far more). Intensive cotton farming depletes and degrades soil fertility, forcing continuous agricultural expansion into natural grassland and forest ecosystems (每件棉衫排放2.1公斤CO₂；棉田耗盡土壤養分並引發鹽鹼化，迫使農業不斷向天然草原與森林棲地擴張).

📌 Stage 1 (2025 Present Baseline): Why Land-Use Change (30%) & Overexploitation (20%) Dominate Today (p.69):
  • 1. Agricultural Expansion (30% Share): Rising global population and affluence mean humanity uses ecosystem resources 50% faster than nature can regenerate them (p.69). Large-scale clearing of tropical forests and grasslands for food and commodities—such as Indonesia’s 3 million ha of palm oil (30-fold increase in 40 years, destroying 80% of Sumatran orangutan habitat) and global cotton plantations—directly removes primary producer habitats (農業與商品開墾佔30%居首：人口增長與富裕消費使人類消耗資源比自然再生快50%，大規模砍伐雨林種油棕與開墾草原種棉花直接摧毀棲地).
  • 2. Direct Overexploitation (20% Share): Intensive overfishing, overhunting, and overharvesting for food, timber, and medicines directly deplete wild populations faster than they can reproduce (過度捕撈、狩獵與伐木佔20%居次).
  • 3. Climate Change & Pollution Today (14% Share): At ~1.2°C–1.4°C warming today, many species can still tolerate conditions or shift locally, while agricultural pesticide/fertiliser runoff (e.g. cotton using 24% of global insecticides) causes localized aquatic toxicity (目前氣候變遷與污染合計佔14%居第三).
📌 Stage 2 (2045 Mid-Century Transition): Why Climate Change (26%) Rapidly Catches Up With Land-Use Change (27%):
  • 1. Positive Feedback from Deforestation & Consumption: Decades of burning tropical rainforests (releasing Biomass carbon) and manufacturing commodities (e.g., 2.1 kg CO₂ released per cotton shirt) accumulate greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, accelerating global warming past 1.8°C–2.0°C (伐林與消費的碳排累積正回饋：長期燒林種油棕與服飾生產碳排累積，加速全球升溫突破 1.8°C–2.0°C).
  • 2. Habitat Fragmentation Blocks Migration: Because agricultural land-use change has already fragmented forests into isolated islands, species trying to migrate poleward or upslope to escape warming temperatures find their corridors blocked by plantations and cities (棲地破碎化阻斷遷徙走廊：前期農墾將森林切割成孤島，使物種無法向高緯度或高海拔遷移避暑).
📌 Stage 3 (2075 Future Projection): Why Models Predict Climate Change (38%) Will Become the Primary Driver (pp.69 & 71):
  • 1. Climate Velocity Outpaces Adaptation (p.71): As textbook p.71 explains, "many species have specific habitat requirements, including temperature, moisture and vegetation types. Therefore, as climate zones shift, some species may be unable to migrate or adapt quickly enough to track suitable habitats, leading to declines or extinctions." (課本 p.71 核心機制：氣候帶移動速度超越物種演化適應與遷移速度，導致大面積物種衰退或滅絕)
  • 2. Global Reach Across Protected Areas: While land-use change can be restricted inside national parks and slowing global population growth may stabilise agricultural land expansion (~24%), atmospheric warming, megadroughts, wildfires, and ocean acidification cross all park boundaries globally (氣候變遷具全球無邊界性：即使設立國家公園限制農墾，大氣升溫、乾旱與野火仍會穿透所有保護區邊界).
🇲🇴 Macau Local Case Study • Evaluating Non-Native Species (連結課本 p.69 Immigrant 90% vs. Alien 42% Risk)

Benefits vs. Ecological Harm of Non-Native & Invasive Alien Species in Macau (以澳門路環山林與路氹城紅樹林濕地之真實物種，剖析外來物種的正面效益與負面生態災難)

🟢 1. Benefits of Non-Native Species in Macau (外來引進種的正面效益) Links to 90% Benign Immigrant Species
🌲 A. Pioneer Afforestation, Slope Stabilisation & Nitrogen Fixation —

In the mid-20th century, Macau’s Taipa and Coloane hills were barren granitic slopes due to historical logging and hill fires. Introduced non-native Taiwan Acacia and Horsetail Beefwood (木麻黃 Casuarina equisetifolia) acted as hardy pioneer trees: their root nodules host nitrogen-fixing bacteria that enriched the poor Gersmehl Soil (S) and Litter (L) stores, prevented severe and landslides, and shaded the ground so native broadleaved seedlings could later establish (正面好處 1：先鋒造林與固氮護坡——早年澳門路環與氹仔山頭荒蕪，引進的外來台灣相思與木麻黃耐旱抗風，根瘤菌能固氮改良貧瘠土壤［增加 Gersmehl 模型中的 S 與 L 養分］，有效防止山泥傾瀉並為後續原生樹種創造遮蔭微氣候).

🌊 B. Rapid Coastal Wave Buffering & Mudflat Accretion —

Originally native to the Bay of Bengal (India/Bangladesh) and introduced around the Pearl River Estuary, fast-growing Sonneratia apetala tolerates polluted tidal water, traps sediment rapidly, and forms a tall coastal windbreak that buffers typhoon storm surges (正面好處 2：初期快速防浪與促淤——外來速生紅樹「無瓣海桑」耐污且生長極快，能在短期內鞏固海堤、消減颱風暴潮並加速灘塗泥沙淤積).

🔴 2. Ecological Harm of Invasive Alien Species in Macau (外來入侵種的嚴重壞處) Links to 42% Endangered Risk (p.69)
🌿 A. "Plant Green Cancer" Smothering Native Forest NPP —

Native to Central/South America, invasive Mikania micrantha aggressively climbs over native trees in Coloane, Ká-Hó (九澳), and Ilha Verde (青洲山). It forms a dense blanket that blocks 100% of sunlight—halting photosynthesis and in native primary producers—and releases allelopathic chemicals into the soil, killing native trees and collapsing local food webs (負面壞處 1：植物界「綠癌」窒息原生林——原產中南美洲的薇甘菊在路環與九澳山林瘋狂攀爬覆蓋樹冠，完全阻斷陽光［切斷原生植物光合作用與 NPP］，導致本土樹木枯死).

🦩 B. Outcompeting Native Mangroves & Shrinking Spoonbill Mudflats —

Despite its initial wave-buffering benefit, Sonneratia apetala has become invasive along Macau’s Cotai Ecological Zone and Taipa coast: its tall, dense canopy shades out native mangroves (such as Kandelia obovata 秋茄/水筆仔 and Aegiceras corniculatum 桐花樹), while its rapid encroachment onto open intertidal mudflats reduces shallow foraging grounds for endangered wintering Black-faced spoonbills (黑臉琵鷺)—forcing Macau’s IAM to manually remove Sonneratia saplings! (負面壞處 2：排擠原生紅樹與壓縮黑臉琵鷺覓食灘塗——無瓣海桑因過度擴張而遮蔽排擠澳門原生秋茄紅樹，並侵佔開闊泥灘，迫使市政署需定期人工拔除).

🐢 C. Pet-Release Predation & Disease (Mirrors Everglades Python p.69!) — &

Just like pet owners releasing Burmese pythons in Florida (p.69), religious "mercy releases" and pet dumping in Macau’s reservoirs and wetland ponds introduced North American (which outcompete native freshwater turtles and spread Salmonella) and South American (which devour wetland macrophytes, lay bright pink egg masses, and alter water quality) (負面壞處 3：棄養放生破壞淡水食物網［與課本佛州棄養緬甸蟒完全同理］——巴西紅耳龜排擠本土草龜，福壽螺則大量啃食濕地水生植物並傳播寄生蟲).

Case Study A • Deforestation, Palm Oil & Nutrient Disruption (pp.68–69)

🇮🇩 Indonesia: Commercial Palm Oil (3M ha) &

🗺️ Google Maps — Sumatra & Kalimantan Palm Oil Belt, Indonesia
📍 1. Geographic Location & Case Foreword (地理位置與案例前言):

Located along the Equator (6°N–11°S, 95°E–141°E) in Maritime Southeast Asia, Indonesia (particularly the islands of Sumatra and Borneo/Kalimantan) lies at the heart of the Sundaland tropical rainforest biome (印尼位於東南亞赤道熱帶雨林帶核心［蘇門答臘島與婆羅洲］，擁有全球最高的淨初級生產力與碳匯，同時也是全球最大的棕櫚油生產國).

🔬 2. Case Situation & Gersmehl Cycle Disruption (課本 p.69 關鍵數據與養分循環衝擊):

✍️ Model Paper 3 Essay Response (Level 3 Full 10 Marks 滿分範文):

In undisturbed ecosystems, original climatic factors—specifically temperature and precipitation—are the primary controls governing Gersmehl nutrient stores and long-term net primary productivity (NPP). As Resource 1A demonstrates, in a tropical rainforest (27°C, 2,800 mm rain), year-round warmth and moisture maximise photosynthesis (NPP = 2,200 g/m²/yr), locking 76% of nutrients (340 t/ha) in the living Biomass (B) store. Because warm, humid conditions supercharge bacterial and fungal decomposers, the decay pathway operates with a residence time of just 0.1–0.3 years; nutrients are immediately absorbed via the uptake pathway, leaving the Litter (L = 3%) and leached Soil (S = 21%) stores small. Conversely, in the Taiga (-20°C winters, 450 mm), freezing temperatures and waxy, acidic pine needles severely bottleneck decomposer activity, extending litter residence time to 18–35 years and making Litter (L = 165 t/ha, 49%) the largest store. In a tropical desert (120 mm rain), extreme aridity restricts NPP to 90 g/m²/yr (B = 5%), yet the virtual absence of rainfall percolation keeps leaching below 0.5 kg/ha/yr, trapping 93% of nutrients (75 t/ha) in the Soil (S) store (第一段結合資料 1A 數據，精準論證原始氣溫與降水如何透過控制 NPP、分解滯留時間與淋溶速率，決定三大生物群系的 B、L、S 自然基線結構).

However, once human land-use disturbances intervene, anthropogenic actions rapidly override and destabilise these climate-controlled cycles. As shown in Resource 1B, commercial palm oil plantations in Indonesia have expanded 30-fold to 3.0 million hectares over the last four decades, destroying ~80% of forest habitat since 2020 and leaving only ~6,600 Sumatran orangutans in the wild. Converting rainforest to monoculture oil palm slashes the Biomass (B) store by −79% (from 340 to 72 t/ha) and reduces natural litter fallout by −68%. Crucially, human deforestation interacts destructively with the original climate: without the multi-layered rainforest canopy to intercept the 2,800 mm of tropical rainfall, surface runoff and downward nitrate/potassium leaching surge by +380% (171 kg/ha/yr lost). To sustain palm yields on the depleted soil, farmers apply 350 kg/ha/yr of synthetic fertilisers and pesticides, which run off into rivers, bioaccumulate in food chains, and poison aquatic fish. Similar human overrides occur when clear-cut logging and acid rain strip Taiga soils, or when overgrazing and cotton irrigation salinise Desert soils (第二段結合資料 1B 印尼 300 萬公頃油棕、生物量暴跌 79% 與淋溶暴增 380% 數據，論證人為土地利用變更如何在短短數年內摧毀氣候平衡，並指出失去樹冠後強降雨反而加速土壤崩潰).

In conclusion, climate is the foundational control over macro-scale, long-term ecosystem potential, dictating the natural equilibrium of the Gersmehl cycle. Yet on a decadal timescale and regional scale, human land-use change is the decisive limiting factor: by removing the critical Biomass store in rainforests or altering soil chemistry in arid and boreal zones, human disturbance turns climate drivers (such as heavy tropical rain) from engines of productivity into agents of rapid nutrient exhaustion (第三段高分權衡結論：區分宏觀長期氣候基線與短期區域人為干擾，指出人為伐林使原本促進生產力的熱帶強降雨轉化為加速養分枯竭的破壞力).

✍️ Model Paper 3 Essay Response (Level 3 Full 10 Marks 滿分範文):

In the present day, there is strong empirical support for the view that rising resource consumption and agricultural land-use change pose the greatest direct threat to global biodiversity. According to Resource 2B, land-use change (primarily for large-scale food and commodity production) is currently the leading direct driver of global biodiversity decline at 30%, followed by overexploitation at 20%. This habitat conversion is driven by rising population and affluence: as Resource 2A shows, humanity currently consumes ecosystem resources 50% faster than nature can regenerate them, with affluent, populous nations such as the USA (8.1 gha/person) and the PRC exhibiting much higher ecological footprints than low-consumption regions like Northern Africa (1.4–1.8 gha). For example, global cotton production (20 million tonnes annually across 90 countries, driven by an average purchase of 60 garments per person per year) clears natural grasslands, diverts 20,000 litres (20 m³) of water per T-shirt and jeans outfit (5 trillion litres/yr for dyeing), and accounts for 24% of global insecticide sales and 11% of pesticides on just 2.4% of the world’s cropland. Although remote sensing technologies (Figure 5.5) can target pest hotspots like the boll weevil to reduce chemical runoff, commodity agriculture—including Indonesia’s 3 million hectares of palm oil and Aral Sea cotton irrigation—remains the dominant terrestrial threat today (第一段支持題目觀點：引用 30% 土地利用變更、50% 資源超載、美中 vs 北非生態足跡，以及棉花 2.4% 耕地消耗 24% 殺蟲劑與 2 萬公升水足跡數據，證明農業開墾與消費是目前最大直接威脅).

However, dismissing invasive alien species underestimates their severe, often irreversible biotic impact on vulnerable ecosystems. Crucially, Resource 2B distinguishes between general non-native immigrant species—90% of which do no obvious harm and can even provide ecological benefits—and destructive invasive alien species, which put 42% of all threatened or endangered species at risk. In 🇲🇴 Macau, for instance, non-native Taiwan acacia (Acacia confusa) was beneficially introduced as a nitrogen-fixing pioneer tree to stabilise eroded granitic slopes, whereas invasive alien Mikania micrantha ("green cancer") smothers native Coloane forest canopies (halting photosynthesis and NPP), invasive Sonneratia apetala outcompetes native Kandelia mangroves and shrinks mudflat foraging grounds for endangered Black-faced spoonbills, and dumped red-eared sliders and golden apple snails disrupt freshwater wetlands. Similarly, in the Florida Everglades (USA), pet-released Burmese pythons attack native American alligators at the top of the food chain and have decimated marsh mammals, proving that in island and wetland ecosystems, alien species can rival land-use change in severity (第二段評估外來物種：精準區分 90% 無害／有益移入種［如澳門台灣相思先鋒造林固氮］與威脅 42% 瀕危物種的外來入侵種［以佛州大沼澤地緬甸蟒攻擊短吻鱷，以及澳門薇甘菊窒息山林、無瓣海桑排擠原生紅樹與福壽螺危害濕地為證］).

Finally, evaluating the statement requires a temporal perspective regarding climate change. Currently, climate change and pollution rank third, accounting for 14% of global biodiversity decline. Yet, as Resource 2B and textbook models emphasise, climate change is predicted to overtake land-use change to become the primary cause of biodiversity loss in the coming decades (38%+ by 2060). Because every cotton shirt also releases 2.1 kg of CO₂ and tropical deforestation releases vast carbon stores, resource consumption and land-use change actively fuel global warming—which shifts temperature and moisture zones faster than stenothermal species can migrate. Therefore, while agricultural land-use change (30%) is the primary direct threat today, climate change represents the overarching, escalating threat of the future (第三段高分時間尺度裁決：指出氣候變遷目前雖佔 14% 居第三，但因消費與伐林同時加劇碳排，預計在 2060 年前後氣候變遷將超越土地利用變更，躍升為全球生物多樣性衰退的首要元兇).
